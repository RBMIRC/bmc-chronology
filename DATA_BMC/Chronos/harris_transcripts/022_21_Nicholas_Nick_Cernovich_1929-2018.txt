The interview was taped in a corridor at the San Francisco
International Airport on the occasion of the 1992 BMC reunion. Nick
and I were both awaiting planes and the corridor was the quietest
place we could find. We were sitting on the floor.

in Arkansas and it no longer existed. And I wrote off to Black Mountain. Berea

Presbyterian—and I found that a little—That did not interest me. That was not

funny little catalog from Black Mountain with a copy of, I think, a lecture by
Schoenberg and this picture of the Studies Building sitting over a lake. And it
