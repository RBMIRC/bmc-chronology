NOTE:
In 1974, I was trying to put together a complex pattern of Black Mountain
activities. I had located a number of music programs, and the first part of the
interview is concerned with various concerts. Although printed programs are a
critical source, it must be remembered that, without doubt, much music was
performed for which there are no programs, and, conversely, there were at times
changes in the programs that are not noted.
The information below is as of this writing:
A program for August 19 [1951] lists a performance by Tudor of Music of
Changes, Part 1.
Two concerts were scheduled for August 9 and 11, 1952. The first on August 9
lists John Cage’s Music of Changes, four parts.
The date for the second concert was changed in ink from August 11 to August
12. [1952]. There are two compositions listed by Cage: Two Pastorales
(premiered in New York, February 10, 1952) and August 12, 1952 (i.e., Water
Music) (premiered in New York, May 2, 1952).
Sonatas and Interludes was performed by John Cage on a visit in April 1948 (the
first complete performance) for which there is no program and on August 16,
1952 (for which there is a small program printed on tissue paper, probably by
Remy Charlip).
Solo Suite In Time and Space by Cage was performed by Tudor and danced by
Cunningham on August 22, 1952.
Tudor was accompanist for Tim LaFarge’s graduation program in dance on
March 9, 1952.
On July 22, 1953 Cages Music for Piano Numbers 1 through 20 was performed.
