MEH: Lou, how did you come to be at Black Mountain College?
LH: John Cage suggested that I be there, so I went down first and had quite an
upset because I'd been for ten years in Manhattan, and to be suddenly in the
country was a bit much for me. And then I went back and did what I could. I
liked it. And then during the time of accustomation, you know, I applied for a
Guggenheim Fellowship and received it and then went to Black Mountain
College simply to live and write, which I did. And I used the little gatehouse
near there as my place. And so, I wrote my first opera there. I must say that it's
one of the few things I've done right in my life. [LAUGHS] I had told the
Guggenheim people that I was going to write an opera and by the time of the
arrival of my last check, I was copying out the last part for the orchestra. And I
