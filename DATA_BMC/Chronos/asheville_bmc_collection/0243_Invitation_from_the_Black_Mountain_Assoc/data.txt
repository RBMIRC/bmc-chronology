formal invitation, woven white paper
The Black Mountain Association invites you to meet Dr. Walter Gropius, Graduate School of Design Harvard University and Mr. Josef Albers Dr. Erwin W. Straus Mr. Theodore Dreier of the faculty of Black Mountain College Black Mountain, North Carolina at the home of Mr. And Mrs. J. Malcolm Forbes 197 Brattle Street Cambridge Friday afternoon, the fifteenth of December at five o’clock R.S.V.P.
