BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE MUSIC INSTITUTE CONCERT
Tuesday August 7 ’45 8:15 pm
Frances Snow Drinker, flute
Emanuel Zetlin, violin
Gertrude Straus, violin
Gretel Lowinsky, viola
Margaret Widelfort, viola
Eva Heinitz, cello
Anna Schauffler, cello
Erwin Bodky, harpsichord
THE MUSICAL OFFERING by JOHANN SEBESTIAN BACH
Fugure in three parts for harpsichord
Five canons
Trio sonata for flute violin and continue cello and harpsichord Largo/ Allegro/ Andante/ Allegro/
Five canons
Fugue in six parts for strings
History and structure of the Musical Offering will be briefly discussed in an introduction by Mr Erwin Bodky.
