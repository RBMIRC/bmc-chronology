paint. I didn't want to do all those other things. Somebody suggested that
maybe a solution would be Black Mountain, where I could concentrate on
painting. Then I found out that they had a summer session, so it seemed like a
way of exploring a possibility for me for later. I knew absolutely nothing about it
except that it was meant to be very exciting and very good, and then I got the
brochure and, well, I took one look at that photograph and Stamos was
