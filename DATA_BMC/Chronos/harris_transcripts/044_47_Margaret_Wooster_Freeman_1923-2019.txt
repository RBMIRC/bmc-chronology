MEH: [GIVES IDENTIFICATION] Margaret, how did you and Alvin come to be at
Black Mountain?
MF: He was a marine who had just come back from the war – the Second World
War. We were married in August '46 and had set up housekeeping in
Providence where he was for a while trying to make a go of helping his father in
an engineering business. But he really hated it. So, I think we'd only been
married a couple of months when I said, "What would you rather do than
anything in the world?" and he said, "Study medieval history." So, he went to
Brown and did a master's there without really much background from his
previous education, and did very well. But he was essentially untried in the
academic field. When he finished, his mentor Barnaby Keeney suggested that
he take one year away from academic [UNINTEL WORD: repardiation?]
Keeney’s uncle had a Black Mountain College tie-in. He had specialized in
collecting outer island, barrier reef, old language, what people were still talking
there at the Outer Banks area. Keeney suggested we go to Black Mountain
College. So, we thought that would be a great adventure, which it was. So, we
were there – That was how we arrived there. It was at a time, as I know you
know, when there was an attempt by an older man – I say "older" and, of
