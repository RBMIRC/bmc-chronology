Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, North Carolina
SECOND MUSIC INSTITUTE, 1945
July 2- September 8 1945
FOURTH CONCERT Saturday, July 28, 8:15 pm
Frances Snow Drinker, flute
Josef Marx, oboe

Gertrude Straus, violin
Gretel Lowinsky, viola
Eva Heinitz, ‘cello

From Canata 156, “Ich steh mit einen fuss”

JOH.SEB.BACH Suite for Violoncello
Prelude/ Allamande/ Counrante/ Sarabande/ Menuet I, II/ Gigue

Allegro moderato/ Allegretto/ Finale
Tuesday, July 31, 8:15 pm, Dr Alfred Einstein will give a lecture by lantern slide on “Mozart’s Handwriting.”
Wednesday, August 1, 8:15 pm, Dr Karl With in his last lantern slide-illustrated lecture on “The Inherent Abstract.”
Saturday, August 4, 8:15 pm, Roland Hayes, tenor, will give his only recital with his accompanied, Reginald Boardman.
