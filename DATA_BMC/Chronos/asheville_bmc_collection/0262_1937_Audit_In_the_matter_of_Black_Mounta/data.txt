William Morse Cole
Professor of Accounting, Emeritus
Harvard University
Cambridge, Massachusetts
November 16, 1937
In the matter of Black Mountain College
To whom it may concern:
I have today completed my fourth annual audit of the accounts of the Corporation of Black Mountain College, covering the transactions of the college year from September 1, 1936, to August 31, 1937. I find the accounts carefully and accurately kept, with due regard for accounting principles. I find not only the income properly accounted for but the expenditures justified by educational policy for an institution of the type that Black Mountain College is. I hereby certify that the accompanying statements (I. Statement of Income and Expenses, and II. Statement of Financial Condition) are in accordance with its books of account as of that date, and, in my judgement, correct.
(Signed) William Morse Cole
