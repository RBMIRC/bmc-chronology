PALMER MEMORIAL INSTITUTE presents A Musical Event of Unusual Interest
*Image of three musicians playing string instruments and piano
A brilliant trio of musicians who have distinguished themselves in the concert halls of Europe; members of the musical faculty of Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, North Carolina who through the special invitation of Dr. Charlotte Hawkins Brown will be guests artists at the Annual Spring Festival of Music SUNDAY, APRIL 30, 1944 3:00 P.M.
CONCERT ADMITTION $1.00 (Tax Included)
100 Seats will be reserved in the order of requests
GENERAL ADMISSION .50c (Tax Included)
Order in advance from: PALMER MEMORIAL INSTITUTE, SEDALIA, N.C.
PROGRAMME
Concerto for two violins and piano in D minor
Antonio Vivaldi (1680-1743)
Gertrude E. Straus
Gretel J. Lowinsky
Edward E. Lowinsky
Fantasia in C minor for the piano
W. A. Mozart (1756-1791)
Nocturne in B-flat minor
Nocturne in F-sharp minor
Frederic Chopin (1810-1849)
Edward E. Lowinsky
Intermission*
Symphony Concertante for violin, viola and piano
W.A. Mozart
Gertrude E. Straus
Gretel J. Lowinsky
Edward E. Lowinsky
*During the intermission at the special request of the artist group the Sedealia Singers will render three numbers.
ARTISTS
EDWARD E. LOWINSKY: Ph.D. Heidelberg, Music historian, pedagogue, teacher in Holland, at Conservatory Stuttgart and Y.M.H.A. Music School, New York. Author of “Book of Children’s Music”, Orlando di Lasso”, “Chromatic Secret Art in the Netherlands Motet” (to be published by the Columbia University Press this year). Black Mountain College since 1942.
GERTRUDE E. STRAUS: Violinist. State Academy of Music Munich Teachers’ Seminar State Academy Munich, Member of String Quartet, North Carolina Symphony Orchestra. Black Mountain College since 1938.
GRETEL J. LOWINSKY: Violinist, violist. Studied in The Hague, Vienna, New York, San Francisco. Member of the North Carolina Symphony Orchestra. Black Mountain College since 1942.
