We will with your help build a dormitory
Black Mountain College is a co-educational liberal arts college. It was started 12 years ago by a small group of teachers and students.
The college is owned by the faculty, and is operated by the faculty and students. It is an attempt to create an intimate atmosphere of working, learning, and living.
The work of building, maintenance, and administration is a mutual responsibility.
This publication was prepared and circulated by the students.
Make checks payable to Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, North Carolina.
Black Mountain College Bulletin Vol. 3, No. 8 August, 1945
Issued seven times a year, in August, September, November, December, January, February and April. Entered as second-class matter November 4, 1942, at the Postoffice at Black Mountain, North Carolina, under the Act of August 24, 1912.
