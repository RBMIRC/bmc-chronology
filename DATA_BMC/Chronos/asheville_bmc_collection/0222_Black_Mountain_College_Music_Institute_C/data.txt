BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE MUSIC INSTITUTE CONCERT
Thursday July 24 ’45 8:15 pm
CAROL BRICE contralto EDWARD LOWINSKY piano
HENRY PURCELL Dido’s Lament: When I am laid in earth
HENRY PURCELL I attempt from love’s sickness to fly
JOHN DOWLAND Come again, sweet love
HENRY FURCELL Nymphs and shepards
J S BACH Two Arias from the B minor Mass Agnus Dei Laudamus
G F HANDEL Three Arias from the Messiah
O, Thou that tellest good tidings
He was despised
He shall feed his flock
BEGRO SPIRITUALS Swing low, sweet chariot – arr by H T Burleigh
Honor, honor I cannot stay here I witness
Arr by Hall Johnson
