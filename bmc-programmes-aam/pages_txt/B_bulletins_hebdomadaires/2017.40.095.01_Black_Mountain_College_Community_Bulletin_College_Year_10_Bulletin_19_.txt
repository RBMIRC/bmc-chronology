BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE COMMUNITY BULLETIN
College Year 10 Bulletin 19
Tuesday, February 23, 1943

CALENDAR
The Internal Finances Committee will meet this afternoon at 2:00 o’clock in Room 10.
The Faculty and the Student Officers will meet in the Kocher Room at 4:30 o’clock.
Classes will be resumed in the morning at 8:15 o’clock.
At the lecture period on Wednesday evening Edward Lowinsky will give the lecture on “Leonardo da Vinci” that was postponed last week.
The concert on Saturday evening, February 27 at 8:00 o’clock will include two compositions by Frederic Cohen: the dance comedy, “Drums in Hackensack”, played by Edward Lowinsky and Frederic Cohen; and the Dance Macabre, “The Green Table”, played by Dorothy Trayer and Frederic Cohen.
The Black Mountain College Radio Program for Sunday, February 28th will be an address on “The Moral Basis” of the “War” by Kenneth Kurtz.

FOR DISCUSSIONS AT FACULTY MEETING:
The appointing of Sam Brown to the Board of Fellows.
The question of admitting third-year high school students to the College.
The time (hour)-evaluation of a student’s work on the course cards.
The question of giving “Ethan Frome” on Friday evening, March 12 and “The Imaginary Invalid” on Wednesday evening, March 17.

ALUMNI NOTE:
Corporal John M Stix is now at Camp Butner, twelve miles from Durham, North Carolina. His address is: US Army, Headquarters and Headquarters Co., 309th Infantry, APC #76.
