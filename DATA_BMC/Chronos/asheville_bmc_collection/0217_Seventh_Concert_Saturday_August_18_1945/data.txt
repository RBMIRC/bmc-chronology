Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, North Carolina
SECOND MUSIC INSTITUTE, 1945
July 2- September 8 1945
SEVENTH CONCERT Saturday, August 18, 1845, 8:15 pm
Emanuel Zetlin, violin
Gretel Lowinsky, viola
Eva Heinitz, ‘cello
Josef Marx, oboe
Erwin Bodky, piano
HUGO KAUDER Sonata in A major for violin and piano
Con moto moderato/ Adagio, Vivace/ Andante, Allegretto
FRANZ SCHUBERT Six Moments for piano, Opus 94
Moderato/ Andantino/ Allegro moderato/ Moderato/ Allegro vivace/ Allegretto
ERNEST BLOCH Meditation Hebraigue for ‘cello and piano
WOLFGANG A. MOZART Quartet for oboe, violin, viola and ‘cello
Allegro, Andante/ Allegro
Wednesday, August 22, 8:15 pm
Robert Motherwell in his first lecture “Artist and Society” (postponed from V-J Day)
Sunday, August 19, 8:15 pm
Lecture by Edward Lowinsky, “Introduction to Huge Kauder’s Work” –performance of Hugo Kauder’s First Piano Sonata, Nine Songs, Slow movement of a viola concerto, Violin sonata.
The Gordon String Quartet will give a series of six concerts on Tuesdays and Saturdays at 8:15 pm during the coming three weeks. Tickets for the series are $5.00, $2.50 for service men and women. Single tickets are $1.25; $0.60 for service men and women. Available at Brown’s Book Store and at the College.
