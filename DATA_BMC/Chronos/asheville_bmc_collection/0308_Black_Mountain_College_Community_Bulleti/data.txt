=== NOTES (Transcription) ===

3p, one sided pages, 3 horizontal folds, staple in top left corner, mimeograph on matte off white paper. Announces that the senior division exam will be on Monday morning at 8:30 am and end on Tuesday afternoon at 6 pm, and an oral trial exam for Marilyn Bauer will be Tuesday afternoon at 3 pm. Students taking senior division exam in March: Curtiss Cowan, Charles Forberg, Ati Gropius, Patsy Lynch, Janet Rees, Anna Schauffler, Nancy Smith. Roxane Dinkowitz and Betty Schmitt will take as practice. Marilyn Bauer will have oral trial exam.
