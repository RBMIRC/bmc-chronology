MEH: [GIVES IDENTIFICATION]. Nancy, how did you hear about Black Mountain?
NSW: I was at school. It was in my junior year at Milwaukee State Teachers College in
Milwaukee. I didn't want to go the fourth year, my senior year. Several of us
were talking about our ideal college, where we would like – someplace we'd
like to go. Somebody said, "Oh that sounds just like Black Mountain." Our
design teacher had visited Black Mountain. So someone said, "Well, let's go
and get a catalogue from the library," and they did. We wrote for information, for
a catalogue. We heard about the Summer Institute. The more I heard, and the
more I read, and the more information I got, the more I wanted to go.
MEH: When you say "we," to whom are you referring?

that conversation, but he did come down that same summer. Hazel and John
and I came down together. Then later, I think the following summer, quite a few
people came from the State Teachers – amazing. Betty Schmitt and her
husband, Pete Jennerjahn; some brothers from – Betty Schmitt and her sister
