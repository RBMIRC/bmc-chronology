=== NOTES (Transcription) ===

Black letterpress on off-white paper, printed on both sides, folded in middle to form program, and again horizontally. 4 copies: copy 1 - partially legible "___ John[?] Slater Helen Slater 15 Raymond St. Cambridge staining, fold marks, tear in original fold copy 2 - corrections: Saturday August 24, 8:15 pm Quintet in E flat major K. 407, for horn, violin, two violas, and 'cello. pasted over original printing, three typewritten notes with corrections and additions tucked inside copy 3, copy 4 laid flat.
