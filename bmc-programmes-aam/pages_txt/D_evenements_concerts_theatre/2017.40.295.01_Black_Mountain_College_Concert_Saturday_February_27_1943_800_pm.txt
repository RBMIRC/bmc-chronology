BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE CONCERT
Saturday, February 27, 1943 8:00 pm
Two Piano Music by Frederic Cohen
“New Amsterdam Music”, the score- embodying five old Dutch folktunes- to Agnes G DeMille’s Dance Comedy “Drums Sound in Hackensack”. First performance, September 1941, Maxine Elliot Theatre, New York.
Edward Lowinsky and Frederic Cohen
Short Intermission
The “Green Table” Suite, music to Kurt Jooss’ Dance Macabre “The Green Table”. First performance, July 1932, Theatre des Champs Elysees, Paris.
The “Gentlemen” around the green table (tango)- Dance of Death (toccata)- the Farewells (marcia)
Death and the Soldiers- the Profiteer and the dead (allegro)
Death and the old Mother (andante)
Death and the Guerilla Woman (allegro)
Death and the Girl in the Brothel (waltz)
Aftermath (marcia funebre)- Death and the Profiteer, Dance of Death (toccata)- the “Gentlemen” still around the green table (tango)
Dorothy Trayer and Frederic Cohen
