your most—

seen a picture of him except in his very, very young days. Knowing the
nature of his work—he is avant-garde and his theories of art and
everything—I felt, well, he would be a big bear of a man, with a beard, and
he would look like he'd just come out of his studio and he would be
brusque and everything. When I met him—and I'm sure you've met him.
He could have been a teller or a trust officer at the bank and was
extremely cordial. I said, "I never met you at that time, but you were there
at Black Mountain College when I used to go to some of those programs."
And we had a very cordial and a very nice conversation.
MEH: You're from Asheville?
