BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE BLACK MOUNTAIN, N.C.
SUMMER SESSION 1949
July 6 to August 31 (eight weeks)

Study
Art: architectural design, painting, drawing
Music: courses in Baroque music, concerts, Bach Festival, instruction
Liberal Arts: philosophy, psychology, languages, science
Workshops: weaving, printing

Practical Experience
Optional program of farm and other outdoor work, maintenance and construction program

Recreation
Mountain hikes in Blue Ridge and High Appalachians, swimming, tennis, cookouts

Community Associations
With members of Black Mountain teaching faculty, outstanding guest faculty, and interesting study body

Program of Visiting Lecturers
Names to be announced at a later date

Inclusive Fee: $380 with same principles of adjustment as apply in fall and spring terms

Inquiries: Registrar, Black Mountain College
