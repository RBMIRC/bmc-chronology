1 page, broadsheet, printed on one side. Announces that the summer session 1951 will stress photography, painting, and dance. Faculty list-- Aaron Siskind, Harry Callahan, Herbert Matter, Hazel-Frieda Larson; Katherine Litz; Ben Shahn and 2 unnamed visiting painters. $400 fee for the session.

There will be a SUMMER SESSION in VITAL CONTEMPORARY ART FORMS stressing particularly PHOTOGRAPHY PAINTING DANCE at BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE JULY 9 to AUGUST 31, 1951
AARON SISKID
HARRY CALLAHAN
HERBERT MATTER
HAZEL-FRIEDA LARSEN
Will teach PHOTOGRAPHY its history stills movies
KATHERINE LITZ will teach DANCE
BEN SHAHN- during the month of July- and VISITING PAINTERS—2 in residence each 2-weeks in August—will teach PAINTING
The fee for the session is $400. A few scholarships—based on need—are available.
Write to the Registrar for an application form; also for more details about the total summer program: Black Mountain College Black Mountain North Carolina
