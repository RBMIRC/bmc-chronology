William Morse Cole
Professor of Accounting, Emeritus
Harvard University
Cambridge, Massachusetts
November 12, 1938
In the matter of Black Mountain College
To whom it may concern:
I have today completed my fifth audit of the accounts and records of the Corporation of Black Mountain College, covering the transactions of its fifth year of existence, September 1, 1937, to August 31, 1938, and I find them kept not only with technical bookkeeping correctness and accuracy, but with due regard for the broad principles of accounting applicable to educational institutions. Income is properly accounted for, and, on the strength of my fifth annual extended residence in the College, I for the fifth time report its expenditures justified by its educational policy. I hereby certify that the accompanying statements (I. Statement of Income and Expenses, and II. Statement of Financial Conditions) are in accordance with its books of account as of the dates indicated, and give, in my judgement, a correct picture of its finances.
(Signed) William Morse Cole
