THESE ARTISTS HAVE CHOSEN TO TEACH AT BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE
CHARLES OLSON...Writer
Charles Olson received his BA and MA from Wesleyan, at Harvard was the first candidate for a Ph.D. in American Civilization, and has twice been a Guggenheim Fellow. He has taught at Clark University, Harvard and Black Mountain. During World War II he was Associate Chief, Foreign Language Division, OWI, and was Foreign Nationality Director of the Democratic National Committee.
His work include:
Call Me Ishmael (Melville critique) ‘47
Y & X (poems) ‘49
The Praises (poems) ‘51
Apollonius of Tyana BMC, summer ‘51
A Letter for Melville 1951 BMC, summer
He has appeared in the following magazines this year:
Origin, #1 & 2; Poetry New York, #3;
Montevallo Review, #1 & 2; Fragmente #1;
Golden Goose #3.
COURSE: in verse: Mayan Hieroglyphic Writing and Ovid’s Metamorphoses.
“For use, now.”
KATHERINE LITZ...Dancer
Katherine Litz was for several years a solo member of the Humphrey-Weidmon company. Her Broadway appearances include “Oklahoma”, “Carousel” and “Ballet Ballads” for which she choreographed “Susanna and the Elders”. Since becoming an independent artist she has presented her own works at the New York City Center, Town Hall and Connecticut College. Teaching: YMHA, Columbia University, Black Mountain and private classes in New York.
Miss Litz composed two new works at Black Mountain this summer, with music by Lou Harrison.
COURSE: Technique and Composition: A training of the body in physical control with emphasis on limitless movement discovery. By this each student may compose dances within his own creative ability.
“The discovery of a way to work is indispensable to creative dance development.”
LOU HARRISON...Composer
Lou Harrison has studied with Cooper, Cowell, Schoenberg, Thomson. He has taught at Mills College, Reed College, Greenwich House Music School. His works have won many awards, and several have been recorded.
His works include:
Symphony in G (in preparation), Suite for Strings #1, Suite for Strings #2, Solstice (Ballet), “Fragment from Calmus” (song), Prelude, Sarabande, Alleluia (for arch.), Trio for keyboard, Orch. Chamber music, solos, songs, ballets, dances, incidental music to plays, percussion music
New music, for dance, Black Mountain, summer ‘51: Chorales for Springs and The Glyph and piece for 2 pianos. COURSE: Composition

Black Mountain College is a small experimental school. Founded 1933 to explore education. A self-owning and self-governing community, without trustees or sponsorship. We do our own maintenance work, and administration. BMC is located in the Craggy Mts., 2400ft., 15 miles from Asheville, near Highway 70. Needs and interests make the curriculum. Though not conventionally graduation oriented, the college gives a BA degree. No grades or credits except for transfer. Work in the creative arts combines with practical work and academic study. The aim is to help the individual to his fullest powers, in himself and with others; to keep the structure open at the top; to grow. For further information write the Registrar, Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, N.C.
