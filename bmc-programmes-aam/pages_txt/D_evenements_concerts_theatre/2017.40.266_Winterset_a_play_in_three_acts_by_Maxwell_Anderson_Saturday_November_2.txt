Performance program for Winterset. Saturday Evening, November 27, 1937. Letterpress on textured offwhite paper.

BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE presents
WINTERSET
a play in three acts by Maxwell Anderson

Saturday evening, November 27, 1937
8:30 o’clock

Act I Scene 1: Bank of a river under a bridge head
Time: Early dark December morning
Scene 2: A cellar room in a tenement house under the bridge
Scene 3: Same as scene 1
Time: Evening of the same day
Act II Scene: Same as act 1, scene 2
Time: Same evening
Act III Scene: Same as act 1, scene 1
Time: Immediately before end of act II
