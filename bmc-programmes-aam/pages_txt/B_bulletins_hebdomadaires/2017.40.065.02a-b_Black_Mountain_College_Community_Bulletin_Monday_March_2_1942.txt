BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE COMMUNITY BULLETIN
Monday, March 2, 1942

Announcements
1 There will be a meeting of the faculty this evening at 7:00 o’clock in the Kocher Room
2 The Board of Fellows will meet after the faculty meeting
3 Jack Kasik will leave tomorrow for Bridgeport, Connecticut, where he will be inducted into military service
4 Ted Dreier will leave on Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon for New York City on a college business trip
5 Bob Wunsch will leave on Wednesday evening for Nashville, Tennessee, where he will confer for two days with the teachers in the negro high schools
6 Mackie Quave of WWNC, will be here this Friday afternoon and evening for the recording and the discussion which was postponed from last Friday
7 Tanya Sprager will present a harp concert Saturday evening at 8:00 o’clock in the Dining Hall
8 The next Sunday evening Radio Program will be a semi-classical violin concert by Ruthabeth Krueger. He accompanist will be Dr Jalowetz
9 Thursday evening, March 12, students in Drama I will present “Suppressed Desires”, a one-act satire by George Cram Cook and Susan Glaspell
10 John Evarts will be in New York City until March 15 when he expects to return to the college. His address: c/o Leslie Katz, 123 East 62nd Street

Alumni Notes
1 Morris Simon has been transferred from the infantry to the medical corps at Fort Meads in Maryland
2 Derek Bovingdon finished his basic training at Fort Knox in Kentucky on January 27. On February 4 he took and passed the exams for the air corps. He expects to be graduated within seven months as a pilot and to hold then the commission of a second lieutenant. He will be transferred to Maxwell Field in Alabama or Kelley Field in Texas

Correspondence
Excerpt from a letter from S Henry Kahn, foreign, general, and technical news correspondent in London:

BMC COMMUNITY BULLETIN, page 2
Monday, March 2, 1942

“I recently saw photographs, published in ‘PW’, of your excellent new building. As Foreign Correspondent for the British Architectural Press this was of the greatest interest to me.
“I feel sure that many British architects would very much like to see more of this construction and I should be obliged if you could have sent to me plans (if possible), photographs, and a description of the building, for publication.
“In return for those I should be very glad to send you copies of the journals containing the articles.”
