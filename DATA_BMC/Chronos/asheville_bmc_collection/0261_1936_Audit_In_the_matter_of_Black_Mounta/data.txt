William Morse Cole
Professor of Accounting, Emeritus
Harvard University
Cambridge, Massachusetts
November 5, 1936
In the matter of Black Mountain College
To whom it may concern:
I have today completed an audit of the financial affairs of the Corporation of Black Mountain College. I have examined its books, its records, and its property, and have lived in its residential hall, eaten in its dining hall, and participated in its activities as far as time would allow during the ten days consumed in the audit. I find its books of account and its records well kept, and its expenditures not only supported by adequate vouchers, but in my judgement, after a lifetime of university teaching, justified by educational policy. I hereby certify that the accompanying statements (I. Statement of Income and Expenses for its second year of operation, ending August 31, 1935, and II. Statement of Financial Condition on August 31, 1936) are in accordance with its books of account as of that date, and, in my judgement, correct.
(Signed) William Morse Cole
