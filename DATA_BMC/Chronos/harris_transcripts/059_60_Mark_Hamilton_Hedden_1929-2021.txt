MEH: [GIVES IDENTIFICATION] Mark, how did you come to be at Black Mountain
College?
MH: My parents knew people at Black Mountain, and I guess the Schaufflers, the
family. So I had heard of it when I was a kid. I think my mother kind of didn't
like the Schaufflers that much or something, and she kind of dissociated that
with the institution. But when I was in college – in prep school – I was trying to
determine a college to go to, and I had alienated myself from Harvard by writing
them a snotty letter. I really didn't want to go there. Felt I should. I thought of
Black Mountain. I got the catalogue, and the images – it was mostly Hazel
Larsen's photographs of the people – and the orientation catalogue just
intrigued me enormously. So I decided – I hesitated. I went, applied to several
other colleges that I thought I should go to. I talked a friend of mine at the
college into applying – Tim LaFarge and then I went.
