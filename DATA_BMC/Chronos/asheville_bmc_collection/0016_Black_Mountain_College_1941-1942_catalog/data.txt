=== NOTES (Transcription) ===

Orange and black letterpress on matte paper. Staples slightly pulling away from front cover. Catalog for the 1941-1942 school years. Image is of front of catalog. A white circular logo is on a bright orange background. Text around the circle reads "BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE BLACK MOUNTAIN N.C."
