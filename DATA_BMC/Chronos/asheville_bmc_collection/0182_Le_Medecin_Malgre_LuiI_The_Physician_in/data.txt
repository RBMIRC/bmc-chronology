BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE Presents Moliere’s “Le Mdecin Malgre Lui” (“The Physician in Spite of Himself”)
A Farce
This play was first performed in 1666
THE CHARACTERS (In order of their appearance)
Sganarelle, a wood-cutter Will Hamlin
Martine, his wife Leslie Paul
M Robert, Sganarelle’s neighbor Erik Haugaard
Valere, a servant of Geronte Danny Deaver
Lucas, another servant of Geronte Mendez Marks
Geronte Ed Kaye
Jaqueline, the nurse in the home of Gail Keith-Jones
Lucinde, Geronte’s daughter Betty Kelley
Leandre, Geronte’s suitor Thomas Brooks
Thibaut, a peasant Francis Foster
Perrin, his son Quintes Dreier
SYNOPSIS
Act I: A Forest
Act II: A Room in Getonte’s House
Act III: A Spot near Geronte’s House
The scenery, the costumes, and the wigs and harts were designed by Frances Kuntz.
The overture and the entr’acte music was composed by John Evarts.
