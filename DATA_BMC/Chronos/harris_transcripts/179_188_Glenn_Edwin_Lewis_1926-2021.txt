Glenn, how did you come to be at Black Mountain College?
GL: Well, I first heard about it through Dan and Jack Rice. I had met Jack at the
camp in Sequoia National Park. We had both worked there. I've forgotten what
Jack was doing, but I played the piano for the evening campfires for a while and
Jack hung around the campfires and that's how we met. I, well, I fell in love with
Jack. Head over heels. I've never been in love with anyone like I was with Jack.
At the time, he married—he met—At the same place, he met Barbara, his wife,
and they became engaged while we were there at Sequoia. That was a year
before. The summer of the next year—again, I'm going to get things confused. I
was in graduate school, and I went down to Santa Barbara, met June and Dan,
and talked them into coming up to Berkeley to go to school. Dan was taking, I
think, some art courses and June was taking some other things. But we lived in
a house that I rented with another couple, James Buzzell Herndon (PH) and his
wife Carol. So, there were five of us living there. Dan and June talked about
Black Mountain. It was obviously the place to go. Jack had gone
there—between when I met him and when Dan and June were in Berkeley
