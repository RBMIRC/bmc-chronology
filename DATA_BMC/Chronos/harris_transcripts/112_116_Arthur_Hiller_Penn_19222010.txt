this abiding kind of Bauhaus atmosphere at Black Mountain. It was enough to
pique my curiosity. That combination of the fact and my own unwillingness to
enroll as a freshman at—I don't know—the University of Pennsylvania, or
somewhere, led me there.

small community, a good deal of freedom, you know, an opportunity for certain
genuine personal inquiries. And that appealed to me a great deal.

women were sent over, actresses, to join these troops so that we could do, you

legitimate plays. We did—I remember I took out a production of Golden Boy,
but there were a number of other serious plays. And so I did that for—oh, I can't
remember now, maybe seven or eight months before I was discharged.
