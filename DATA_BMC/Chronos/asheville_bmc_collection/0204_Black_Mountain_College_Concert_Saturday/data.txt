BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE CONCERT
Johannes Brahms (1833-97)
Sonata in G-Major for violin and piano, opus 78
Vivace ma non troppo
Adagio
Allegro molto moderato
Trudi Straus, Heinrich Jalowetz

Sonata in F-Major for piano and violin, opus 24
(Spring Sonata)
Allegro
Adagio molto expressivo
Scherzo (allegro molto)
Rondo (allegro ma non troppo)
Gretel Lowinsky, Edward Lowinsky
Saturday April 17, 1943 8:00 PM
*handwritten in pencil on the back
“A Criticism of James Joyce’s Dubliners
In this land
Where nothing materializes and
The telling phrase tells nothing or
Nothing more than….
Within reason or without a doubt that
Doubtless we move
From the promise to the expected
“Daybreak, Gentlemen!”
We should be thus
Economical with life
Bring neither too kind nor to unjust
And not enough….
Ralph Tyler Jr,”
