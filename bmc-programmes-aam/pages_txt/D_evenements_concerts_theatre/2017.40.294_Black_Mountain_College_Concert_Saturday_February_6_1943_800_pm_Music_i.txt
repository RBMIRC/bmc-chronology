BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE CONCERT
Saturday, February 6, 1943 8:00 PM
Music in Mediaeval Society
Introduction with lantern slides
Monodic Music
Gregorian Kyrie and Gloria- in Festis Duplicibus (De Angelis probably 9th century
Can cei la lauseta mover- Troubadour song Bernart de Ventadorn (12th c.)
Nu alerat lebe ich mir werde- Crusadesong (1228) Walther con der Vogelweide
Main so levoit Aaliz- Rondeau Abelard (?) (1079-1142)
Gregis paster Tityrus- Song of the young clergymen for the feast of the “asses”- 12th century
En mal la rousee que nest la fler- Jongleur song 13th century
Medieval dance music: a) Ductia 13th century, b) Estample 13th century
III.	Polyphonic Music
IV.	Punctus fundamentallis, Le moulin de Paris with variation) for organ Anonymous (early 15th century) Buxheimer Orgelbuch (second half of the 15th century)
Sederunt Principes- Organum quadruplum, Per seguir la speranca- Ballata Perotin (about 1200) Francesco Landini (d.1397)
De plus en plus se reneuveilie- Chanson Gilles Binchois (d. 1460)
Walhereux cueur que veux tu faire- Chanson Guillaume Dufay (1400-1474)
Der walled ist entlawbet Lechheimer Liederbuch (1452)
Or sus, vous dormes trop, ma dame ieliete- Morningserenade Anonymous (14th century)
Sumer is icumen in- Rota Anonymous of Reading Abbey (about 1240)
