BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE COMMUNITY BULLETIN
Monday, February 23, 1942
I.Announcements
1 The following students are taking the Senior Division Examinations
a) for entrance into the senior division:
Robert Bliss, Betty Brett, Sam Brown, Danny Denver, Rudy Haase, Gisela Kronenberg, Paeo Leon, Jane Robinson, Claude Steller
b) for practice:
Renate Benfet, Dora Harrison, Peter Hill, Marjorie Karlin, Ed Kaye, Lise Kulka, Marjorie Wensch, Nan Oldenburg, Leslie Paul, Carolyn Spencer, Jacqueline Tankersley
2 The faculty will meet this evening at 7:00 o’clock in the Kocher Room for the short session
3 There will be a general meeting in the dining hall at 6:30 o’clock this evening. The meeting will adjourn in time for the Roosevelt speech
4 There will be a fire drill sometime during the week. Detailed instructions for the drill will be posted on the bulletin board
5 Mackey Quave, WWNC announcer for the Black Mountain programs, will be at the college Friday evening to make recordings for people who are interested—musical or voice—and to talk with with those students who are interested in radio production. Details will be posted
6 On Saturday evening, beginning promptly at 8:30 o’clock, there will be a concert followed by a production of Woliere’s “The Highbrow Ladies”. Invitations have been sent to a hundred or more people in Western North Carolina, and a general invitation to the people of Black Mountain will be put up in the Post Office
7 The next Sunday evening College radio program will be the postponed Mark Twain broadcast, if copyright on Twain’s writing can be waived
II Alumni Notes
1 The February show at the Boston Art Museum includes paintings by Harrison Begay, Indian student at the college last year. He has been one of the most successful Indian artists and hi new work is said to surpass anything he has exhibited previously. Harrison has enlisted in the army and will leave shortly for his post
BMC Community Bulletin, February 23, page 2
2 Aviation Cadet Bela Martin has been transferred from New Orleans to the US Naval air station in Pensacola, Texas
(Alumni notes will become a regular feature of the Bulletin)
III Excerpt from a letter from Mrs JB Jamieson
“I am leaving a week behind at BMC fascinating to me from every viewpoint and I am grateful for the privilege of being a guest. The vision and problems for your faculty seemed raised to the n’th degree at this time, but I have faith, too, that a wise solution will evolve from your very necessities. BMC has friends who believe in its ideals. I hope they will rally to help your crucial need. Whatever ones near Boston I can contact, I will tell of my visit and understanding of it. Please let me know in what ways I can further help, small though they will be….
“Your students, faculty, and whole community gave me a welcome for which I wish to express sincere thanks.
“I await, with impatience, the developments in plans for the college for the summer and the future.”
IV For Discussion at Monday evening meeting
1 Jerry Wolpert’s graduation: a written report on Jerry’s examinations has been received by the college from Stringfellow Barr
2 Senior Division Plans of
Will Hamlin
Mendez Marks
3 Plans for reading the Senior Division Examinations. Suggestions will be appreciated
V For Discussion at next meeting (which ought to be held soon)
The summer plans of the College
