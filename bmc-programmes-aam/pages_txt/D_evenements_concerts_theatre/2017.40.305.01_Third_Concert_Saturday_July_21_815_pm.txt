Black Mountain College, Black Mountain, North Carolina
SECOND MUSIC INSTITUTE, 1945
July 2- September 8 1945

THIRD CONCERT Saturday, July 21, 8:15 pm
Emanuel Zetlin, violin
Erwin Bodky, piano
W. A. MOZART Sonata in E-flat major for Violin and Piano
Allegro molto/ Adagio/ Allegretto
F. BUSONI Sonata No. II Ops. 36a for Violin and Piano
Andante/ Presto/ Andante piuttosto grave, Andante con variazioni (played without interruption)
FR.SCHUBERT Duo Op. 162 in A-major for Violin and Piano
Allegro moderato/ Scherzo/ Andantino/ Allegro vivace
Wednesday, July 25, 8:15 pm
Dr. Karl With in his third lantern slide-illustrated lecture on “Evolving Needs in Form.”
Saturday, July 28, 8:15 pm
J.S. Bach: Three Sinfonias from cantatas with oboe and strings, Solo suite for ‘cello, Sonata for flute, violin, and harpsichord.
J. Haydn: Trio for piano, violin and ‘cello.
For detailed concert programs see Saturday Citizen and Times
Each Tuesday at 8:15 pm there will be an informal concert, for which admission tickets are obtainable at the college, at $1.00, 0.50 for servicemen
