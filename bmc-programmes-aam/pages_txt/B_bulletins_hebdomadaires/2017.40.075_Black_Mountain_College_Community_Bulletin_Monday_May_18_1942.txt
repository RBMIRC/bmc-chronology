BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE COMMUNITY BULLETIN
Monday, May 13, 1942

Schedule
Monday: There will be a meeting of the Faculty this evening at 7:00 o’clock in the Kocher Room.
Tuesday: Students in dramatics will give Synge’s “Riders to the Sea” in the lobby of North Lodge at 8:30 o’clock.
Thursday: There will be an important general meeting in the dining hall at 8:00 o’clock.
Saturday: The 1942-1942 spring semester ends.
Announcements
Peter Bergmann is now a citizen of the United States. He received his papers on May 6.
There is an exhibit of Frances Kuntz’s costume plates, stage designs, and theoretical studies in room 115, formerly the Babcock’s apartment.
Worton Steinau will post today or tomorrow on the bulletin board directions for storing books and furniture for the summer.
Faculty Meeting
Consideration of reports of the examiners.
Report on the mathematics test (Bergmann)
Conclusion of the term-end reports on students by the advisors.

1p, one sided. Mimeograph on matte off white paper. Announces Peter Bergmann's citizenship, and a Frances Kuntz's exhibit.
