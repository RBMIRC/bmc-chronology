don't think we're going to have too much anymore except highways or something
like that. I feel, just myself, that if I'm divorced from nature, from looking say at
that ivy crawling up that tree, which is not a good thing for the tree – But if I ever
get in a place where I can't see those things, I'm going to feel terribly deprived,
and aesthetically sort of hollow. Because it's – I like texture and I like the colors of
nature and I like the flowering of nature, so if I had to be taken from that I don't
think I would be working, although I work in an abstract fashion. But it comes
from what's outside.
