William Morse Cole
Professor of Accounting, Emeritus
Harvard University
Cambridge, Massachusetts
November 16, 1939
In the matter of Black Mountain College
To whom it may concern:
I have today completed my sixth annual audit of the accounts and records of the Corporation of Black Mountain College, covering the transactions of its sixth year of existence, September 1, 1938, to August 31, 1939, and I find them kept not only with technical bookkeeping correctness and accuracy, but with due regard for the broad principles of accounting applicable to educational institutions. Income is carefully accounted for, gifts have been scrupulously applied to the purposes for which they were given, and, on the strength of my sixth annual extended residence in the College, I for the sixth time report its expenditures justified by its educational policy. I hereby certify that the accompanying statements (I. Statement of Income and Expenses, and II. Statement of Financial Condition) are in accordance with its books of account as of the dates indicated, and give, in my judgement, a correct picture of its finances.
(Signed) William Morse Cole
