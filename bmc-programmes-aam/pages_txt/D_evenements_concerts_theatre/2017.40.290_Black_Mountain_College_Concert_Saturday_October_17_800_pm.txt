BLACK MOUNTAIN COLLEGE CONCERT
Saturday, October 17 8:00 pm
Sonata in G Major for piano and violin WA Mozart
Gretl and Edward Lowinsky
Rondo for violin from the Haffner Serenade WA Mozart
Trudi Straus and Heinrich Jalowetz
Duo in B Flat Major for Violin and Viola WA Mozart
Trudi Straus and Gretl Lowinsky
Hofbaltaenze (dances for a gala ball at the Austrian Imperial Court) Joseph Lanner arranged by F Cohen
John Evarts and Frederic Cohen
Saturday evening, October 24, at 8:00, there will be a farewell concert of music by John Evarts. The program will include his overture and incidental music to Moliere’s “The Physician in Spite of Himself”, three of his songs from “Let me Have Air”, and a number of piano portraits.
The following Saturday, October 31, the College will present a program of piano music. Frederic Cohen, Heinrich Jalowetz, and Edward Lowinsky will ply music by Purcell, Mozart, and Brahms.
